# Bounded scientific verification and collaboration agenda

Each collaboration concerns one object, one falsifiable question and one reviewable deliverable. A failed replication, counterexample or prior-art match is an admissible result.

| Object | Available status | Priority task | Stop or revise if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim-transition corpus | Public 21-event snapshot and structural tools | Independent coding of at least 6 of 21 events, disagreements kept, then external corpus | Coding is not reconstructible or agreement is insufficient under a predeclared rule |
| Delta_isolate | Proof reported internally; no autonomous proof in this public package | Produce a mathematical-only note, independent review, prior-art search | A lower-bound gap or counterexample is found |
| G30 spectral fibres | Finite results documented; exact autonomous certificate to consolidate | Supply exact definitions and witnesses, review fibre statement | Exact discrepancy or scope counterexample |
| Barning–Hall 17/330/2310 | Autonomous certificates announced in later documents, absent here | Obtain witnesses, replay independently, audit normalization and relative minimality | Replay fails or depends on an unstated convention |
| HOL-01 p=7 | Separate public finite certificate v1.1.1 | External replication and comparison with classical theory | A witness fails or the asserted scope exceeds p=7 |
| HOL-02 and congruence extensions | Private finite laboratories; not a public uniform theorem | Written bridge, prior-art review and tests fixed before extension | Integrity failure or explicit conjecture falsifier |
| Prime-race covariance | Conditional theoretical framework and numerical claims in an internal report | Supply exact normalization, source code, zeros or L-function calculations and outputs | Witnesses unavailable or theory/measurement mismatch |

No global endorsement of the program is required. Roles and credit follow actual contributions, using CRediT where useful. A task starts from a genuinely available public artefact; objects still absent from this repository first require an autonomous mathematical extract or package. Major open problems, including RH, remain horizons and must be reduced to explicit bounded sub-problems.
