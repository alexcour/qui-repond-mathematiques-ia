## Truth and novelty

Reclassification as classical does not make a true theorem REFUTED. Truth/warrant, novelty, diffusion and documentary condition are separate questions. The frozen schema has four state axes and does not yet include a dedicated novelty axis. A schema extension must be versioned explicitly.

## HOL2 as later context

For odd primes p, reduction SL₂(Z/p^(n+1)Z) → SL₂(Z/p^nZ) splits only when (p,n)=(3,1). The first-level obstruction for p≥5 is already given by Stasinski, §6, Theorem 6.2; that section refers to Sah for the classical classification. No general novelty is claimed. This contextual note does not establish when every claim was first made and does not add a retrospective event to the frozen corpus.

Reference: A. Stasinski, Representations of SL_n over finite local rings of length two, Journal of Algebra 566 (2021), 119–135. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2020.08.036 ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.09878
